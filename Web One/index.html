Engagement Lifecycle

A structured, predictable 5-step implementation.

Every project begins with a clear audit and a strictly scoped pilot—defining inputs, outputs, permissions, success criteria, and operating costs before any live deployment.

01
Fit Call 30 Minutes • Exploratory

A focused conversation between our technical lead and your operations team to understand your current inquiry volume, repeat customer questions, and existing software stack. We determine whether an AI-assisted workflow is genuinely beneficial for your business.

Outcome: Clear determination of technical feasibility and workflow suitability.
02
Workflow Audit 1 – 2 Weeks

A thorough teardown of your customer intake paths, documentation gaps, and internal handoff friction. We map where staff spend repetitive time and identify exact boundaries for AI assistance versus human judgment.

Outcome: Architecture blueprint, verified knowledge corpus inventory, and fixed pilot scope document.
03
Scoped Pilot 2 – 4 Weeks

We engineer and deploy the initial solution in a controlled staging environment. Every pilot explicitly defines its operational boundaries before launching to visitors:

1. Verified Inputs: Approved documentation & structured intake fields only.
2. Deterministic Outputs: Grounded answers & formatted triage drafts.
3. Explicit Permissions: Restricted API scopes; no direct database overwrites.
4. Success Criteria: Target answer accuracy >95%; complete handoff logs.
5. Operating Costs: Hard monthly token & hosting caps configured upfront.
6. Human Review Points: Mandatory approval gates for all client-facing messages.
04
Evaluation & Handover 1 Week

We evaluate pilot metrics against baseline benchmarks, test edge-case handling, conduct staff training sessions, and transfer full repository code, documentation, and credential ownership to your team.

Outcome: Production release, team training, full asset transfer, and runbook delivery.
05
Optional Ongoing Support Monthly • Flexible

Periodic model updates, prompt tuning, knowledge base refreshes as your service offerings evolve, latency monitoring, and new tool integration scoping as your agency expands.

Outcome: Retained architectural oversight without vendor lock-in.

Empirical Verification

How improvements are measured, not assumed.

We do not publish fabricated case studies, stock customer logos, or inflated ROI claims. Instead, we establish baseline metrics before launch and track concrete performance indicators throughout the pilot.

Inquiry Handling Time Efficiency

The total staff minutes required from initial visitor inquiry submission to sending a personalized, qualified response.

Before Pilot Manual triage, repetitive typing: ~25–45 min per lead
After Pilot Target Pre-structured draft review: < 5 min per lead
Handoff Completeness Data Quality

Percentage of inbound inquiries containing all necessary scoping details (company size, current tech stack, primary workflow objective) upon first touch.

Before Pilot Generic form fields with 40% missing context
After Pilot Target Structured qualification capture exceeding 90%
Reviewed-Answer Quality Accuracy

Audit score of assistant responses evaluated against owner-approved knowledge documentation for factual correctness and brand tone.

Before Pilot Unassisted or ungrounded chatbot drift risks
After Pilot Target Strict doc retrieval with >98% grounding adherence
Exception Rate Reliability

Frequency of out-of-distribution queries, API timeouts, or unanswerable requests gracefully routed to human review without visitor disruption.

Before Pilot Silent dropped inquiries or generic error walls
After Pilot Target 100% of ungrounded queries captured in fallback log
Customer-to-Human Rate Conversion

Percentage of qualified website conversations that successfully transition into scheduled discovery calls or approved email correspondences.

Before Pilot Static form bounce rate often exceeding 80%
After Pilot Target Direct calendar booking & transparent intake path
Ongoing Operating Cost Unit Economics

Actual monthly expenditures on LLM tokens, cloud hosting, and integration servers compared against staff time saved.

Before Pilot Expensive recurring SaaS seats with unused features
After Pilot Target Predictable utility token billing (~$15–40/mo typical)

Empirical Standards Notice

Performance improvements are measured empirically for each client engagement based on agreed baseline logs. AIVIBEDEV does not publish guarantees or universal percentages that have not been validated in your specific operating environment.
