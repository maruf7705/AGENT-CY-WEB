Architecture & Engineering

The technology stack explained in business terms

We believe engineering clarity builds trust. Here is how each component in our architecture functions, why we chose it, and how it protects your business operations.

Interface Layer Web Standards & Semantic HTML

Website & Staff App

The front-facing interfaces that customers and internal team members actually use. Engineered with semantic HTML5, modern CSS, accessible keyboard navigation, and fast-loading modular JavaScript.

Business Value:

Ensures high customer conversion on mobile devices and eliminates friction for operations staff reviewing incoming leads.

Reasoning & Knowledge Grounded Retrieval Boundaries

LangChain

Governs assistant behavior, knowledge indexing, schema validation, and tool access. We use LangChain to ensure assistants only answer questions using verified, owner-approved documents.

Business Value:

Protects company reputation by preventing hallucinations, fabricated turnaround times, or unauthorized pricing commitments.

Stateful Orchestration Multi-Step Stateful Graphs

LangGraph

Provides controlled, stateful processes when workflows involve multiple sequential steps, cyclic reviews, specialist agent routing, or complex validation checkpoints.

Business Value:

Maintains reliable state memory and audit logging across complex multi-stage tasks without losing context or dropping requests.

Integration Layer Self-Hosted / Cloud Automation

n8n

Connects your website and AI workflows directly into your existing business tools (HubSpot, Notion, Slack, Airtable, Google Workspace) via structured webhooks and reliable retry policies.

Business Value:

Eliminates manual copy-pasting between systems while keeping your team inside the tools you already know and pay for.

Model & Infrastructure Selection Policy

We do not force a single AI model provider or proprietary tool onto your business. Models (such as Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet / Haiku, OpenAI GPT-4o / GPT-4o mini, or specialized open-weights models) are chosen based strictly on your latency requirements, privacy needs, and monthly token budget.

Every client account connects directly to their own provider API keys—ensuring you maintain direct billing, full data privacy, and zero agency markup on token usage.

Technology Evaluation Notice: Hermes Agent

Hermes Agent is evaluated strictly as an open-weights autonomous agent technology for appropriate internal use cases and high-throughput research workloads. We do not imply an official commercial affiliation with its creators, nor does it power every client deployment. Model architectures are chosen based on your exact cost, security, and privacy requirements.

"We start with the simplest architecture that meets the requirement. More agents are not automatically better."
