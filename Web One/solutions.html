Connected AI Websites

A bespoke website coupled with a customer-facing assistant grounded strictly in your verified company documentation.

Most B2B websites are digital brochures: visitors arrive with specific scoping questions, face generic contact forms, and wait days for a basic qualification email. A Connected AI Website engages high-intent prospects immediately—answering approved questions accurately and capturing complete scoping parameters.

Grounded Retrieval: Answers derived strictly from approved FAQs, whitepapers, and service tiers.
Structured Intake: Collects budget, timelines, and current stack with explicit visitor consent.
Human-in-the-Loop: All proposals and direct outbound emails pass through human review gates.

Connected Operations

Workflows that organize inbound information, route inquiries to the right team members, and enforce human approval checkpoints.
