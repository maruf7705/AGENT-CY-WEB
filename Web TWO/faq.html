Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on architecture, safety, and ownership

Everything you need to know about how our systems connect, what your team owns, and where human judgment remains in control.

It is a complete system. While a customer-facing assistant is visible on the website, it is tightly connected to your underlying business tools (like HubSpot, Notion, Slack, and Airtable) and a dedicated internal staff review interface. It answers repeat questions from approved documentation, gathers structured inquiry requirements, and brings the details directly into your team's workflow with human approval checkpoints.

Yes. While we build bespoke Connected AI Websites from the ground up, we can also integrate grounded assistants, n8n webhook pipelines, and staff triage portals into your existing website (WordPress, Webflow, Next.js, etc.) and software stack. We adapt to the tools your team already uses rather than forcing you to migrate.

No. By default, our systems are built with human-in-the-loop review checkpoints. The AI assistant answers informational questions and structures inquiry data, but significant operational actions—such as dispatching proposals, charging cards, modifying master CRM records, or publishing contracts—require explicit confirmation by your team members in the staff interface.

Multiple agents are only deployed when a task genuinely involves distinct specialist roles, separate knowledge partitions, or complex multi-stage asynchronous validation (e.g., one agent parses legal filings while another validates financial numbers against a database). For standard customer questions and lead capture, a well-grounded single-agent architecture with structured tools is faster, more reliable, and significantly cheaper to run.

The assistant is strictly constrained to your owner-approved knowledge base. When asked a question outside that verified corpus, it clearly acknowledges its boundary and offers a direct inquiry form or scheduling link so a human team member can answer. It never invents policies, pricing, or turnaround times.

Your team owns 100% of the deliverables: website source code, n8n workflow export files, LangGraph orchestration scripts, grounded markdown knowledge files, and documentation. Everything runs on standard web infrastructure and your own direct cloud accounts—meaning zero vendor lock-in.

The only ongoing costs are direct infrastructure fees paid directly to providers: static web hosting (typically $15–$25/month on Vercel/Netlify), model token consumption (typically $5–$30/month for average B2B traffic via Anthropic/OpenAI API), and any existing software subscriptions you maintain. There are no mandatory ongoing agency licensing fees unless you choose an optional retainer for continuous workflow optimization.
