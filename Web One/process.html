Our 5-Stage Implementation Process

From initial assessment to production handover: how we design, build, test, and transition your AI systems with total transparency.

01
Stage 1: Fit Call 30 Minutes • No Cost

We meet with your founder or operations lead to review your current customer intake process, weekly inquiry volume, and team handoffs. We identify whether an AI-assisted website or operations system will produce genuine operational savings or if existing off-the-shelf tools suffice.

Key Input: Current website URL, tools in use, core bottlenecks.
Key Output: Candid go/no-go recommendation for AI augmentation.
02
Stage 2: Workflow Audit 1 – 2 Weeks

We conduct an in-depth audit of your customer-facing communication and internal handoffs. We review past email transcripts, repetitive client questions, knowledge base documents, and API compatibility across your software stack.

Key Deliverable: Knowledge readiness report & edge-case boundary map.
Architectural Spec: Pilot blueprint detailing the exact minimal viable stack.
03
Stage 3: Scoped Pilot 2 – 4 Weeks

We build and deploy the solution in a staging environment. Before a single live visitor interacts with the system, our pilot agreement rigorously defines the six operational governance pillars:

1. Strict Inputs: Approved documentation corpus and validated form fields only.
2. Verifiable Outputs: Citations to source documents; formatted triage payloads.
3. Scoped Permissions: Read-only document access; webhook endpoints with scoped API tokens.
4. Success Criteria: Pre-agreed accuracy benchmarks, intake completion rate, and speed.
5. Operating Costs: Hard monthly spend caps on LLM API tokens and cloud instances.
6. Human Review Gates: Mandatory operator review for any high-impact outbound communication.
04
Stage 4: Evaluation & Handover 1 Week

We stress-test edge cases, verify fallback behaviors, train your operations team on reviewing triage items, and transfer complete source code, repository access, and infrastructure credentials directly to your team.

Asset Handover: 100% source code, n8n workflows, and documentation runbooks.
Team Training: Live 1-on-1 walkthrough for your operations staff.
05
Stage 5: Optional Ongoing Support Monthly • Cancel Anytime

For teams that want ongoing peace of mind, we provide retained support covering proactive prompt optimization, model version upgrades, vector database maintenance, and new tool integration scoping as your business scales.
