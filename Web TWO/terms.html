Commercial Terms

Terms of Engagement

Our standards regarding project scoping, intellectual property handover, infrastructure billing, and warranty commitments.

1. 100% Client Intellectual Property Handover

Upon final project acceptance and milestone settlement, all bespoke website source code, integration automation workflows, and customized documentation are assigned 100% to the client. You own the code completely.

2. Direct Infrastructure Billing

Clients maintain their own direct billing relationships with third-party infrastructure and API providers (such as Vercel, Netlify, Anthropic, OpenAI, and n8n Cloud). AIVIBEDEV does not charge hidden markups on model token usage.

3. Scoped Pilot Governance

Every implementation includes a formal Scoped Pilot phase where inputs, outputs, permissions, quality metrics, and human review gates are verified in a staging environment before public launch.

4. No Guarantees of Revenue or Traffic

While we optimize websites and workflows for conversion and operational efficiency, we measure empirical operational improvements rather than making unverified revenue or traffic guarantees.

5. Inquiries & Modifications

Custom terms of service and enterprise master services agreements (MSAs) are provided during formal proposal scoping. Contact us at inquiries@aivibedev.com.
