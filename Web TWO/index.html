Architecture & Stack

The technology stack explained in business terms

We use reliable, production-tested software tools. We select components based on your team's specific business requirements rather than forcing trendy frameworks into every project.

Interface Layer Web Standards / Responsive

Website & Staff App

The clean, accessible web interfaces that your customers and internal staff actually interact with. Fast-loading, fully responsive, and built for usability on mobile and desktop devices.

Business Function: Delivers high-conversion inquiry pages for clients and streamlined review consoles for your operations team.
Reasoning & Retrieval Grounded Knowledge

LangChain

Governs assistant behavior, knowledge indexing, tool access, and retrieval boundaries. Ensures the assistant only answers using verified documentation approved by your team.

Business Function: Eliminates hallucinations by strictly grounding AI responses in your verified company FAQs and service matrices.
Stateful Orchestration Multi-Step Logic

LangGraph

Provides controlled, stateful execution processes when your work involves multiple sequential steps, cyclical reviews, specialist verification components, or complex branching logic.

Business Function: Maintains audit logs, state memory, and predictable recovery paths across multi-stage business workflows.
Integration Layer Workflow Automation

n8n

Connects your website and AI assistants directly to your existing operational tools—such as HubSpot, Notion, Airtable, Slack, and Google Workspace—with self-hosted privacy and reliability.

Business Function: Automates cross-tool synchronization without forcing your team to migrate away from existing software.

Technology Evaluation Notice: Hermes Agent

Hermes Agent is evaluated strictly as an open-weights autonomous agent technology for appropriate internal use cases and high-throughput research workloads. We do not imply an official commercial affiliation with its creators, nor does it power every client deployment. Model architectures are chosen based on your exact cost, security, and privacy requirements.

What Every Scoped Pilot Defines Before Public Launch:

To protect your brand and operational stability, every pilot establishes six mandatory operating guardrails prior to receiving live traffic:

1. Verified Knowledge Boundaries Approved documentation corpus that the assistant is permitted to reference.
2. Tool Permissions & Access Restricted webhook endpoints with zero write access to private financial data.
3. Human Decision Gates Clear triggers where AI pauses and hands control to designated staff.
4. Success & Quality Criteria Measurable response quality standards and handoff completeness targets.
5. Monthly Token Run Rates Pre-calculated token usage budgets and hosting cost ceilings.
6. Exception Handling Workflows Structured escalation paths for unexpected queries or server timeouts.

Proof & Measurement

Empirical metrics established before and after launch

We do not publish fabricated logos, unverified reviews, or hypothetical revenue guarantees. Instead, we define exact operational baselines and measure improvements methodically.

Inquiry Handling Time

The time elapsed between a visitor asking a question and receiving a verified answer or scheduled consultation.

Measurement Protocol: Baseline response latency vs. post-launch automated triage duration.

Handoff Completeness

The percentage of incoming leads containing all necessary scoping details (stack, team size, timeline) before staff review.

Measurement Protocol: Rate of required fields populated without requiring manual back-and-forth emails.

Reviewed-Answer Quality

Adherence of assistant answers to approved company documentation, verified by internal operations spot-checks.

Measurement Protocol: Human review score on knowledge retrieval accuracy and citation precision.

Exception Rate

The frequency of unanswerable or ambiguous queries that trigger safe fallback handoffs rather than hallucinations.

Measurement Protocol: Logged count of boundary fallbacks vs. total conversation sessions.

Customer-to-Human Handoff Rate

The proportion of interactions where high-intent prospects transition smoothly to a booked call or designated partner.

Measurement Protocol: Completed scheduling conversions relative to qualified inquiry sessions.

Ongoing Operating Cost

Exact monthly expenditures on infrastructure, model tokens, and database webhooks monitored against project budgets.

Measurement Protocol: Monthly billing receipts from direct API providers compared against initial scoping.
Transparency Standard: Improvements are measured systematically across these six operational dimensions. We never guarantee generic percentage gains without establishing a formal baseline during your workflow audit.
