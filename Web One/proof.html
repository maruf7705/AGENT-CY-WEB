Our Measurement Framework

Why we don’t rely on generic marketing hype or fabricated testimonials, and how we measure real operational success.

The Six Core Operational Indicators

During the Workflow Audit, we establish your organization's exact baseline across these six parameters, then measure them rigorously throughout the pilot.

1. Inquiry Handling Time Staff Efficiency

Total operational minutes spent between visitor inquiry submission and dispatch of a personalized, qualified response.

Current Baseline Manual review & drafting: ~30 min per lead
Post-Pilot Target One-click approved triage: < 5 min per lead
2. Handoff Completeness Data Quality

The percentage of inbound leads containing all necessary scoping parameters (budget range, timeline, stack, explicit consent).

Current Baseline 40% of leads require follow-up emails for basic details
Post-Pilot Target >90% complete structured intake upon first arrival
3. Reviewed-Answer Quality Grounding Accuracy

The percentage of public AI assistant answers verified to match owner-approved documentation without ungrounded hallucination.

Current Baseline Risk of generic AI drift or missed inquiries
Post-Pilot Target >98% verified doc citation adherence
4. Exception Rate Robustness

The percentage of unanswerable or edge-case queries that are gracefully logged and escalated to human staff.

Current Baseline Visitor abandonment on unhandled questions
Post-Pilot Target 100% of out-of-scope queries logged for review
5. Customer-to-Human Rate Conversion Rate

The conversion percentage of high-intent website visitors who successfully book a discovery call or request a custom proposal.

Current Baseline Static form abandonment rate (~80% drop-off)
Post-Pilot Target Interactive qualification & immediate calendar booking
6. Ongoing Operating Cost Unit Economics

Direct monthly expenditure on model tokens and infrastructure compared to staff hours reclaimed.

Current Baseline Bloated per-seat SaaS tools with low adoption
Post-Pilot Target Utility token billing (~$10–$35/mo per volume)

No Fabricated Guarantees

We believe in honest, empirical engineering. Operational improvements vary depending on your agency’s inquiry volume, service complexity, and existing processes. We measure real data points before and after deployment so you have verifiable proof of performance.
