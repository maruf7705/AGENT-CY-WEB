Engagement Process

A measured, low-risk deployment methodology

We work in five clear stages so your team maintains complete visibility over technical scope, permissions, operating costs, and quality criteria before going live.

01
Exploratory Call

Fit Call (20 Minutes)

A focused discussion with our engineering lead to examine your current website, typical inquiry volume, customer repeat questions, and existing software stack (HubSpot, Notion, Slack, Airtable, etc.).

Outcome: A frank assessment of whether an AI website or connected operations system will solve your bottleneck, with an honest recommendation.
02
Diagnostic Phase

Workflow Audit

We analyze your existing customer intake paths, compile the verified documentation corpus for grounded retrieval, map tool permissions, and identify the exact points where human staff must review decisions.

Outcome: Detailed integration architecture diagram, knowledge inventory, and projected monthly operating token run-rate.
03
Development & Sandboxing

Scoped Pilot Build

We build the custom website, assistant retrieval adapter, intake schemas, and n8n webhook routes in an isolated staging environment. We define and test all six pilot boundary guardrails prior to public exposure.

Pilot Guardrails: Inputs, outputs, permissions, quality thresholds, operating budgets, and human review gates are locked before deployment.
04
Quality Benchmark & Handover

Evaluation & IP Handover

We measure pilot performance against our six empirical quality metrics, conduct staff training on managing the knowledge files, and transfer complete source code, repository access, and webhook configurations to your team.

Outcome: 100% intellectual property ownership transferred to your team with zero vendor lock-in.
05
Optional Retainer

Optional Ongoing Support & Optimization

For firms that want continuous iteration, we offer optional monthly optimization retainers to update knowledge files, refine intake schemas, expand tool integrations, and audit model costs.

Outcome: Proactive monthly workflow maintenance without mandatory lock-in commitments.

The 6 Mandatory Pilot Guardrails (Established Before Live Traffic):

1. Verified Knowledge Boundaries Approved documentation corpus that the assistant is permitted to reference.
2. Tool Permissions & Access Restricted webhook endpoints with zero write access to private financial data.
3. Human Decision Gates Clear triggers where AI pauses and hands control to designated staff.
4. Success & Quality Criteria Measurable response quality standards and handoff completeness targets.
5. Monthly Token Run Rates Pre-calculated token usage budgets and hosting cost ceilings.
6. Exception Handling Workflows Structured escalation paths for unexpected queries or server timeouts.
