Architecture & Engineering

How We Build: The Stack in Business Language

We select engineering tools for reliability, security, inspectability, and ease of handover—not for marketing hype.

Layer 01 • Touchpoints

Website & App Interfaces

The fast, intuitive interfaces your visitors and internal staff interact with every day.

We build client-facing websites and internal operations portals using clean, semantic web standards (HTML5, responsive CSS, lightweight JavaScript, or modern React frameworks when application state demands it). We prioritize sub-second load times, mobile ergonomics, readable typography, and strict WCAG AA accessibility compliance.

Why it matters to your business: High-friction or slow websites cause qualified buyers to bounce before asking a question. Our frontends load instantly and guide buyers effortlessly into a conversation.
Layer 02 • Reasoning & Knowledge

LangChain: Controlled Agent Behavior & Retrieval

Grounded knowledge retrieval that prevents unapproved statements and hallucinated pricing.

LangChain provides the scaffolding for structuring prompts, formatting tool inputs, and executing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). By grounding your assistant in verified vector embeddings of your service handbook, pricing sheets, and policies, the model can only answer from owner-approved facts.

Why it matters to your business: Brand safety. If a visitor asks an unanswerable question or attempts prompt injection, LangChain enforces strict retrieval fences and falls back to a polite human handoff.
Layer 03 • Stateful Multi-Step Control

LangGraph: Controlled, Cyclical Workflows

Deterministic state machines for complex tasks requiring multi-step verification and human pauses.

When a business process has multiple sequential dependencies—such as parsing an RFP document, checking internal consultant capacity, querying a CRM, and generating a proposal draft—standard linear prompts fail. LangGraph allows us to build stateful graphs with memory, cyclical error-checking loops, and mandatory human checkpoints.

Why it matters to your business: Total auditability. Every state transition is recorded in persistent logs, allowing your team to pause, inspect, and approve tasks before they execute.
Layer 04 • Integrations & Middleware

n8n: Transparent Workflow Automation

Connects website interactions directly to your existing business stack without vendor lock-in.

n8n is an enterprise-grade, node-based workflow engine that can be self-hosted on a low-cost private server or run via managed cloud. We use n8n to connect website events to your CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce), team communication (Slack, Teams), project tracking (Notion, ClickUp), and custom databases.

Why it matters to your business: No SaaS lock-in and zero per-seat fees for your operational team. Your data stays on your infrastructure, and you have complete visual visibility into every webhook.
Layer 05 • Model Selection & Internal Research

Pragmatic Model Selection

Selecting the right LLM for latency, accuracy, and cost—never forcing a single provider.

We benchmark across leading providers—including Anthropic Claude 3.5 Sonnet, OpenAI GPT-4o / GPT-4o-mini, and open-weights models (Llama 3, Mistral)—to choose the most cost-effective and accurate model for each sub-task. Fast, small models handle qualification and metadata extraction; deep reasoning models handle complex synthesis.

Evaluation Note on Hermes Agent:

Hermes Agent is evaluated as an emerging technology for specific internal reasoning and open-weights automation use cases. AIVIBEDEV maintains no formal commercial affiliation with Hermes, and we do not claim it powers every deployment. We deploy it only where benchmarked performance proves superior for your specific workflow.
