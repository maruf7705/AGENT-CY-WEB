Proof & Measurement

Empirical operational measurement

We do not publish fabricated client logos, unverified testimonials, or hypothetical revenue guarantees. Instead, we establish exact operational baselines and measure improvements methodically.

1. Inquiry Handling Time

The total duration between a prospect landing on your website with a question and receiving a verified answer or confirmed consultation slot.

Measurement Protocol: Baseline manual email turnaround vs. post-launch automated triage duration.

2. Handoff Completeness

The percentage of incoming inquiries that arrive with all necessary scoping information (tech stack, timeline, team size, budget) before staff review.

Measurement Protocol: Number of complete structured intakes vs. vague one-line form submissions.

3. Reviewed-Answer Quality

The precision with which assistant answers match approved company documentation, verified by regular internal operations spot-checks.

Measurement Protocol: Human review audit score on knowledge retrieval accuracy and citation integrity.

4. Exception Rate

The rate at which unanswerable or ambiguous queries are safely routed to human staff rather than generating inaccurate answers or hallucinations.

Measurement Protocol: Count of logged boundary fallbacks vs. total conversation sessions.

5. Customer-to-Human Handoff Rate

The proportion of interactions where high-intent prospects successfully transition to a booked call or designated partner conversation.

Measurement Protocol: Completed scheduling conversions relative to total qualified inquiry sessions.

6. Ongoing Operating Cost

Exact monthly expenditures on infrastructure, model API tokens, and database automation webhooks tracked against initial project scoping.

Measurement Protocol: Monthly billing receipts from direct API providers compared against initial scoping.
Transparency Standard: Improvements are measured systematically across these six operational dimensions. We never guarantee generic percentage gains without establishing a formal baseline during your workflow audit.
