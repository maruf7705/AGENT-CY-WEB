Experience both sides of a Connected AI Website: the visitor qualification experience on the left, and the internal operations team review triage on the right.

How This Flow Operates in Production

When deployed for your business, this simulation is connected directly to your approved knowledge base and existing operations software:

1. Knowledge Grounding

Vector embeddings of your approved PDFs, service guidelines, and pricing tiers ensure the assistant never invents unapproved offers.

2. Webhook Dispatch

When a visitor submits an inquiry, an n8n webhook securely structures the contact, logs explicit consent, and formats a triage card.

3. Human Approval Gate

Your operations lead reviews the AI-generated draft in Slack or an internal portal. One click dispatches the tailored proposal.
